Do I need a watch?

No. Every member can score 100 a day by hand. A watch that writes to Apple Health — Polar, Garmin, Oura, Apple Watch — scores training, sleep, steps and mindful minutes for you, up to 150.

What does it read from Apple Health?

Sleep, workouts, steps, active energy and mindful minutes. Optional; nothing is read until you allow it, and you can turn it off.

Is the trial free?

14 days, everything unlocked, through the App Store. It renews at 8,99 € a month or 89,99 € a year unless you cancel in Settings before it ends.

What does the coach know?

Your check-ins, your training logs and your Apple Health sleep — and only once you switch AI on.

Android or iPad?

iPhone only.
