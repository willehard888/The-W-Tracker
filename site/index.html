The daily ritual Scroll

A day has four marks.

Hit them and the day counts. Miss them and that shows too.

  1. 06:30 Sleep

    The night counts. With Apple Health on, it is read, not typed.

  2. 07:15 Training

    A session built from the muscles, the minutes and how hard. Loads follow what you lift.

  3. 12:30 Fuel

    Search it, scan the barcode or photograph the plate. Measured against targets set from your profile.

  4. 21:00 Lock in

    One check-in, under a minute. The streak moves, the rank moves, the coach reads it.

Status is earned. There is nothing to buy.

Everything hangs off the check-in.

The Coach
Reads your check-ins, training and sleep before it answers. A morning brief, a plan for the day, a review every week. Off until you turn it on.
